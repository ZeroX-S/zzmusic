# Unofficial community client notice

ZZ Music is an independent, unofficial community client. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by or sponsored by Tencent or QQ Music. QQ Music and related names and marks belong to their respective owners.

The application provides read-only discovery, account-library viewing and playback workflows. It does not provide password login, manual Cookie editing, account-write operations, media downloads, DRM circumvention or service credentials.

QR login runs in the existing Qt 5 process. Candidate credentials are kept in memory until remote validation succeeds, then the accepted session is stored through Sailfish Secrets.
