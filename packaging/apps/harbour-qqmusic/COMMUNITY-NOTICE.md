# Unofficial community client notice

harbour-qqmusic is an independent, unofficial community client. It is not
affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tencent or QQ
Music. QQ Music and related names and marks belong to their respective owners.

The application exposes only the read-only and playback workflows documented
by this project. Availability depends on the user's region, account rights,
network, and the upstream service. It does not provide media downloads,
account-write operations, DRM circumvention, or service credentials.

Existing session data is restored and stored through Sailfish Secrets. This
build does not provide an interactive sign-in flow. Session data is never
included in diagnostics, notifications, queue snapshots, or public caches.
